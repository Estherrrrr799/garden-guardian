# 🌿 Garden Guardian
### A Two-Player WebAR Puzzle Adventure

A magical storm has shattered the fairy village. Two guardians must follow different paths, uncover hidden clues, and work together to rebuild what was lost.

**Garden Guardian** is a browser-based AR game prototype that combines garden exploration, role-based puzzles, and face-to-face collaboration.

👉 **[Open the prototype](https://estherrrrr799.github.io/garden-guardian/)**

## ✨ The Experience

Two players explore the garden using their own phones. Each takes a different role and discovers clues along a separate route.

- **Player A — Detective:** Follow clues from the pine tree to the left bench.
- **Player B — Scout:** Investigate the dead stump and continue to the right bench.

The experience is designed around sharing information: each player holds part of the puzzle, so communication is essential to completing the adventure.

## 🎮 Intended Gameplay

1. **Choose your role**  
   Open the game on two phones and select Player A and Player B.

2. **Scan and explore**  
   Find the designated image markers and scan them to reveal AR elements, story prompts, and puzzles.

3. **Exchange clues**  
   Follow your individual route, then describe your clues to your partner to solve a shared constellation puzzle.

4. **Rebuild the village**  
   Meet at the centre of the lawn and work together on the final rune-sequencing challenge.

A fairy companion provides hints and feedback throughout the journey. The game includes an energy-star system and countdown penalties for incorrect answers.

## 📱 What You Need

- Two players, each with a camera-equipped smartphone.
- An internet connection and a mobile browser with camera access.
- The designated game markers and printed constellation reference card.

**This is an image-tracking AR experience.** Opening the website alone is not enough to complete the game: players also need access to the matching markers and supporting play materials.

Players coordinate in person and progress on their own devices.

## 💡 Design Focus

Garden Guardian explores how AR can encourage people to engage with both a physical place and another player.

- **Different roles:** Separate routes give each player a distinct responsibility.
- **Shared problem-solving:** Partial clues encourage conversation and collaborative reasoning.
- **Environmental storytelling:** Trees, benches, and the lawn become locations within a fairy-tale adventure.
- **Guided exploration:** A fairy companion, progress indicators, and visual feedback help players navigate the experience.

## 🛠 Built With
